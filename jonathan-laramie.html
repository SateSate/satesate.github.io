Associate

Jonathan Laramie, Esq.

Attorney Jonathan Laramie has been with the firm for over 8 years and represents clients on a variety of issues, including estate planning, probate of estates, and filing estate tax returns; guardianships and conservatorships; real estate transactions; income tax returns for individuals, trusts, estates, and partnerships; and business law.

Jonathan earned his B.A. at the University of Connecticut and his J.D. from Suffolk University Law School. He also completed an internship as a law clerk in the Superior Court Judicial system.

Legal Assistant

Sabrina Mazzeo

Sabrina is the head of the client service team at Lui & Frasca. Her responsibilities include assisting firm attorneys with document preparation. She prepares deeds in the transfer of real estate to trusts and third parties, homesteads, and various recordings at the registry of deeds.

Sabrina also maintains close client contact and oversees scheduling and follow-up to be sure all necessary documents are complete, and that clients receive executed document copies in a timely manner. Sabrina holds a Bachelors of Psychology from Southern New Hampshire University and is currently working on her paralegal certification.

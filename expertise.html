Practice Areas

Expertise

A highly experienced firm with a comprehensive approach. Our primary practices include family wealth and estate planning, trust and estate administration, taxation, tax-exempt organizations and private foundations.

What We Do

Counsel for every chapter

From the first plan to the most complex transition, we support individuals, families, and closely-held businesses through every stage of personal and business life.

Estate & Trust Wealth Transfer Planning

Our attorneys counsel individuals, entrepreneurs, family businesses, and families of substantial wealth regarding estate planning and wealth preservation, as well as business succession issues. We facilitate clients' comprehensive planning goals — minimization of taxes, international planning, charitable gifts, retirement planning, asset protection, marital property planning, and structuring family business transactions.

  • Wills
  • Revocable Living Trusts
  • Marital Trusts

Estate & Trust Administration

We represent and counsel executors and trustees in the administration and management of complex estates and trusts, as well as fiduciary litigation and other controversies. We are also counted upon to serve as trusted executors and trustees at the request of our clients.

  • Fiduciary Services and Trusteeships
  • Estate tax return filings

Taxation

Our attorneys are trained, educated, and experienced in all aspects of taxation. We prepare income tax returns for individuals, trusts, estates, corporations, LLCs, and other partnerships, and we routinely prepare estate and gift tax returns. We see the big picture — the interplay between estate, gift, and various income taxes — and work to reduce taxes for our clients and their families.

  • Tax return preparation and compliance
  • Tax audit representation
  • Tax litigation and controversy

Business Law

Counsel for closely-held companies and the families who run them — from formation through succession. We help structure entities, negotiate agreements, and protect intellectual property at every stage of the business lifecycle.

  • Entity formation: corporations, LLCs, LLPs, Limited Partnerships
  • Trademarks, Servicemarks

Elder Law and Medicaid Planning

We assist individuals, couples, seniors, Veterans and their spouses and families with Elder Law, Medicaid Planning, Estate Planning, Wills, Trusts, Powers of Attorney, Special Needs Planning, Veteran's Benefits, and Real Estate.

  • Irrevocable Trusts
  • Life Insurance Trusts
  • Education Trusts
  • Disability & Handicap Persons Trusts

Fiduciary Services

We are counted upon to serve as trusted executors and trustees at the request of our clients — bringing institutional rigor and a deep understanding of the families and businesses we support.

  • Fiduciary Services and Trusteeships
  • Estate tax return filings
